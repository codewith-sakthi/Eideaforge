About Eideaforge

Our Mission

Eideaforge was conceived to bridge the gap between academic innovation and real-world venture creation. We empower students across departments to brainstorm novel ideas, recruit talented teammates, participate in structured pitch competitions, and receive direct evaluation and feedback from faculty experts.

Through multi-round hackathons and rubric-based evaluations, students build essential entrepreneurial, technical, and pitching competencies.

🚀 For Innovators

Publish your ideas, form cross-disciplinary teams, get feedback, and compete for incubation seed support.

🧑‍⚖️ For Evaluators

Score pitch decks, prototypes, and presentations seamlessly with weighted rubric criteria and qualitative notes.
